---
title: Play by touch in the browser
category: feature
release: 0.2.0
targets: []
credit: [Gunnar Beutner]
---

A touch screen can now do more than tap. A tap is a left click, a tap over a movie stops the
movie, dragging one finger pans the map under it, pressing and then dragging opens the usual
selection band, a two finger tap is a right click, and dragging two fingers pans the map
whatever the first finger was doing. Only the tap worked before: the map could not be panned
at all without a hovering pointer, and a movie could only be stopped with the escape key.
A movie the mission forbids skipping is still not skippable. Mouse and keyboard play is
unchanged.
