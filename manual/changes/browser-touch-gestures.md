title: Play by touch in the browser
category: feature
release: 0.2.0
targets: []
credit: [Gunnar Beutner]
---

A touch screen can now do more than tap. One finger is the left button — tapped, held as a
drag, or pressed and held, which is the right button and so the game's cancel and deselect —
and two fingers dragged pan the map, with a two finger tap kept as a second way to reach the
right button. A touch during a movie still stops the movie, and one the mission forbids
skipping is still not skippable. See [Touch controls](/systems/touch-controls/).

Only the tap worked before this release cycle: the map could not be panned at all without a
hovering pointer, and there was no way to reach the right button, cancel a structure waiting
to be placed, or deselect. Mouse and keyboard play is unchanged, and a device carrying both
answers to either at any moment.
